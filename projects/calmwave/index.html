CalmWave

Physiology-Based Music Generation System for Intelligent Stress Management
基于生理建模与音乐生成的智能压力干预系统
Figure 1. The closed-loop architecture for physiology-based music generation.

An intelligent closed-loop regulation process:
Perception → Recognition → Intervention → Feedback

智能闭环调节过程：感知 → 识别 → 干预 → 反馈

The system leverages the MusicGen large-scale music generation model to create personalized intervention music.

• Under high-stress conditions, it generates upbeat and energetic tracks to facilitate stress relief.
在高压状态下，系统生成激昂活力的音乐以辅助解压。

• Under low-stress conditions, it produces calming and smoothly melodic music to maintain positive mood.
在低压状态下，系统生成舒缓优美的旋律以维持积极情绪。

Through real-time physiological monitoring and user feedback, the system continuously fine-tunes parameters, ensuring dynamic and effective stress management. 通过实时生理监测与用户反馈，系统持续微调参数，确保动态有效的压力管理。
